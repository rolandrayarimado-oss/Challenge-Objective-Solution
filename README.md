Justification

I made the decision based on the information available at the time, prioritizing the meeting that appeared to have the greater immediate business impact and considering the difficulty of rescheduling external stakeholders. However, I recognize that the executive’s priorities may not always be obvious from the calendar alone. In hindsight, I should have confirmed the preferred priority with the executive before moving either high-priority meeting, unless there was an established scheduling rule to follow.

Improvement

I would create and maintain an Executive Meeting Priority Guide that documents which types of meetings should take precedence, preferred attendees, meetings that can be delegated, and acceptable rescheduling windows. This would allow me to make faster decisions while staying aligned with the executive’s preferences.
