2. Inbox Organization

I would not attempt to read all 120+ emails in chronological order. I would quickly triage them into these categories:

Priority 1 – Urgent/Executive Action: Investor issues, major clients, deadlines today, legal/financial matters, travel disruptions, or issues requiring the executive personally.
Priority 2 – Important/Delegation: Important business requests that can be handled by a team member or require follow-up.
Priority 3 – Routine: Non-urgent requests, scheduling confirmations, updates, and general correspondence.
Priority 4 – Waiting/Follow-Up: Emails where action has already been taken and I am waiting for a response.
Archive/Spam: Marketing, irrelevant messages, newsletters, and spam.
Three Key Email Responses

1. Urgent client request

Hi [Client Name], thank you for reaching out. I’ve flagged this as a priority and am reviewing it with the appropriate team now; I’ll follow up with an update as soon as possible.

2. Scheduling request

Hi [Name], thank you for your message. I’m checking the executive’s availability and will confirm the most suitable meeting time shortly.

3. Routine request that can be delegated

Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out. I’ve forwarded your request to the appropriate team member for review and will make sure it is followed up on.
