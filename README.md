Calendar Conflict

I would first review both meetings to determine which is more time-sensitive, which attendees are difficult to reschedule, and whether either meeting can be delegated or shortened. Since both are high-priority, I would avoid making the decision without the executive's input and immediately present the best options.

Recommended solution: Keep the investor meeting as scheduled if it involves funding, financial decisions, or multiple external stakeholders, and request a revised time for the key partner meeting. If the partner meeting is equally critical, I would explore having a trusted senior team member attend on the executive's behalf.

Good morning. We have a conflict between the investor meeting and the key partner meeting at the same time.
